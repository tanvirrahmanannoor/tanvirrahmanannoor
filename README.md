### 👋 About Me
I am an Agriculture undergraduate specializing in satellite remote sensing, spatial modeling, and agro-environmental data analysis. Using **Google Earth Engine (JavaScript API)**, **ArcGIS Pro**, and **QGIS**, I work with multi-sensor satellite and climate datasets to map flood inundation, drought dynamics, soil salinity, land surface temperature, watershed hydrology, and land-use suitability.
